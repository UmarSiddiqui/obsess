Momo Tycoon

मोमो व्यापार

Your little steamer stall needs customers. Cook momos, serve hungry locals and tourists, then grow the stall into a busy Newari restaurant.

  • Tap a steamer to load it, and tap again when the momos are ready.
  • Tap a customer's order bubble (or the customer) to serve them.
  • Upgrade steamers, unlock jhol, C-momo and fried momo, hire staff.
  • Pay wages and rent every payday. Keep your reputation up.
